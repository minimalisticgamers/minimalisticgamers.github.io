What We Behold / Privacy · Effective 31 October 2026

Your story stays here.

The app runs locally. No app account, advertising or analytics service is enabled.

Scope.

This policy applies to What We Behold, the independent MinimalisticGamers iPhone fan port of We Become What We Behold. It covers the app and these support pages.

Local game data.

The app does not send gameplay choices, stored progress, preferences, identifiers or backup files to the developer. It uses local web storage where supported by the original game. Gameplay resources are bundled. A local HTTP connection to 127.0.0.1 serves those resources inside your own device; it does not send them to an internet server.

The short story runs as one live session. There is no original persistent mid-story save. Restarting begins a fresh session; native web-storage export is not a full session checkpoint.

Backups and deletion.

If you export a backup, you choose where Files saves or shares it. A selected cloud-storage provider handles a file under its own policy. Import validates the supported format and asks before replacing local data. Use the app’s reset controls to clear its web storage, or remove the app to delete its local app data. Keep any backup you need first. We do not keep a server copy.

Apple and TestFlight.

Apple processes App Store, TestFlight, device and diagnostic information under its policies and your device/account choices. If you send TestFlight feedback or diagnostics, Apple may share your submitted feedback and diagnostic information with the developer through Apple’s services.

Support and this website.

The GitHub support tracker is optional and public. GitHub processes your account information, posts, attachments and technical access data under its policies. Share only the details needed to resolve your issue. These static pages are hosted by GitHub Pages, whose infrastructure may process standard request information such as IP address and browser details. The pages use no analytics, advertising scripts, forms or third-party fonts.

External links and questions.

Links to the original creator or another provider open only when you choose them. That provider’s privacy terms apply. Ask general privacy questions through the support tracker without posting personal information; GitHub manages deletion/access requests for information it holds.
