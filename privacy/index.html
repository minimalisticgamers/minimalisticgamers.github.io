About this policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026

This policy explains how MinimalisticGamers handles information in our games that link to this policy and on this website. For privacy questions, contact contact@minimalisticgamers.io.

Offline games and local saves

Our games work offline and do not require an account. Game progress and settings are stored on your device. We do not operate game servers or receive your saves through normal gameplay.

Our games do not run advertising or analytics services and do not track you across apps or websites. We do not collect advertising identifiers or sell personal information.

Where a game offers save export or import, it reads or writes the file you choose. A storage provider you select may synchronize exported files according to its own settings and privacy policy. Device or iCloud backups may also include app data, depending on your settings. Our games do not provide their own cloud accounts or automatic cross-device save synchronization.

Managing your game data

You can use a game's reset controls, where available, to reset progress, or delete the app to remove its local app data. Exported saves and existing device or cloud backups are separate; manage or delete them through the relevant file, device, or backup settings. We cannot retrieve or delete a save stored only on your device.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message, and any information or attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond, resolve support issues, and maintain necessary support records. Email is processed by the services used to send and receive it. Please avoid sending sensitive information or files unrelated to your request.

We retain correspondence only as long as needed for those purposes or to meet applicable legal obligations. You can ask to access, correct, or delete information you have sent us by emailing contact@minimalisticgamers.io. We will handle requests as required by applicable law.

Apple services and beta testing

Apple processes information for App Store distribution, device backups, and diagnostics under its own policies and your device settings. If you choose to share app diagnostics with developers, Apple may make crash or diagnostic reports available to us. We use reports we receive to investigate problems and improve our games.

If you test a beta through TestFlight, Apple automatically collects and shares crash logs and usage information with us and may include your name and email address. Feedback you submit through TestFlight is also shared with us. We use this information only to improve our apps and do not share it with third parties. These platform services are separate from our games' offline save storage.

We keep diagnostic reports and beta feedback only as long as needed to investigate issues and improve our games. You may contact us about information we hold. Records held by Apple remain subject to Apple's retention practices and available privacy controls.

Learn more in Apple's Privacy Policy and TestFlight & Privacy.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising, tracking cookies, or contact forms. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes and may process technical request information to provide and protect its hosting service. This processing is described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. GitHub's GitHub Pages documentation explains its visitor IP logging.

External links

If you open a link to another website or service, that provider handles information under its own privacy policy. Opening a support email link uses your chosen email app.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy if our practices change, including before introducing features that handle information differently. The effective date above identifies the current version. For questions or privacy requests, email contact@minimalisticgamers.io.
