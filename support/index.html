A little context helps.

If you’re writing about a game, please include:

  • The game’s name and version.
  • Your device model and operating system version.
  • What happened and what you expected.
  • Steps to reproduce the issue, and a screenshot if useful.

Please share only the information needed to help. Leave out passwords, payment details, and other sensitive information.

About your save.

Games with saves keep their progress locally. We don’t have a server copy of your save. If a game offers export, keep a separate backup before resetting progress or removing the app.

Privacy questions.

Use the support tracker for general privacy questions without posting personal information. You can edit or delete your own posts where GitHub allows; GitHub handles requests about information it holds under its own privacy policy. Read our shared privacy policy for details.
