A little context helps.

If you’re writing about a game, please include:

  • The game’s name and version.
  • Your device model and operating system version.
  • What happened and what you expected.
  • Steps to reproduce the issue, and a screenshot if useful.

Please share only the information needed to help. Leave out passwords, payment details, and other sensitive information.

About your save.

Our games store progress locally. We don’t have a server copy of your save. If a game offers export, keep a separate backup before resetting progress or removing the app.

Privacy questions.

Use the same email for questions about your information or to request deletion of support correspondence. You can also read our shared privacy policy.
