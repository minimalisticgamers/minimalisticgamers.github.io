No account to create. No ads to interrupt. Your progress stays on your device, with save export and import when you want a backup.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Gameplay works without an internet connection. Your workshop and its save live on your device.

Does production continue when I’m away?

Production runs while you play. Closing the app pauses it, so your workshop waits for you to return.

How do I keep a backup?

Open the app’s native Settings to export a save file. You can import that file later. Keep a separate backup before resetting progress or removing the app.
