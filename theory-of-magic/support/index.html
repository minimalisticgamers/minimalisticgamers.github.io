Theory of Magic / Support

A little help.

Backups, offline play and help with your iPhone game.

Player support

Tell us what happened.

Open support on GitHub

A GitHub account is required. Posts and attachments are public; leave out personal details and private save files.

Keep a separate backup.

Use native Settings to export or import a full wizard-hall backup through Files. The original Get Save control also offers its compatible save text through the native file controls.

Do not remove the app until you have a separate backup. We do not have a server copy of your progress.

Playing offline.

Original stored-time and Fast-forward mechanics are preserved. Keep a separate backup before reinstalling. No account, cloud service, advertising or in-app purchase is required.

Report a problem.

Include the game name, version/build, iPhone model, iOS version, what happened and steps to reproduce. A screenshot can help; check it for personal information before posting.

Edition and availability.

An independent iPhone adaptation of the original Theory of Magic / Arcanum game. The iPhone edition is in preparation and is not available to download.
