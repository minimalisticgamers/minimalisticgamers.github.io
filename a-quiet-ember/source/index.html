The original A Dark Room

A Dark Room is by Michael Townsend, doublespeak games, and contributors. The original game’s rules, story, translations, and credits remain part of this adaptation.

Play the original browser game · Read the original source

The source ZIP also includes the preferred editable translation files, template, and converter from pinned upstream commit 1fada462. The bundled hosted runtime remains separately preserved with its recorded hashes and differences. You can also download that complete upstream source archive.

A Quiet Ember is an independent iOS adaptation by MinimalisticGamers. It is separate from the official A Dark Room mobile edition and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the original creators.

Building the iOS host

Open ADarkRoom.xcodeproj in Xcode and choose the ADarkRoom scheme. The app supports iOS 17 or later. The native source includes touch controls, a responsive local game view, and user-initiated save import and export. A signing team and a bundle identifier you control are needed for a device build.

The included source README records the packaged version and reproduction steps. The source download does not include developer credentials or grant rights to the original game’s trademarks.
