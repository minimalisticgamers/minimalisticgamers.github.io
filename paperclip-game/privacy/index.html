Privacy information for the Paperclip Game iPhone app (com.skylar.paperclipgame).

IN THIS POLICY

Local game dataLinks and supportApple servicesManaging your data

Effective 29 October 2026

MinimalisticGamers does not collect personal data through normal gameplay. The app has no account, advertisements, tracking, or gameplay analytics.

Game data stays on your device.

The original game and its required resources run locally. Game progress, settings, and a native save backup are stored inside the app on your device. We do not operate a game server or receive your save through normal play. The native host blocks remote game resources, including upstream analytics.

Export reads your save and writes a file to a destination you choose. Import reads the file you select. A storage provider you choose may synchronize exported files under its own settings and policy. Device or iCloud backups may include local app data according to your Apple settings.

External links and support.

Links you select open outside the game. The destination service processes your visit under its own policy. Support is through public GitHub issues. If you post, we and other visitors can see your username, public profile, message, and attachments. Use support only for information needed to investigate a problem, and do not post personal details or private saves. Public posts can remain visible after an issue closes; GitHub provides controls to edit or delete your own posts.

Apple services and TestFlight.

Apple handles App Store services, device backups, and optional diagnostics under its policies and your settings. If you choose to share diagnostics, Apple may make reports available to us to investigate problems. TestFlight automatically collects and shares crash logs and usage information with the developer and may include your name and email address. Feedback submitted through TestFlight is also shared with us. We use reports and feedback we receive to investigate issues and improve the app, and keep them only as long as needed for those purposes.

See Apple’s Privacy Policy and TestFlight & Privacy.

Managing your data.

Use the game’s reset controls where available, or delete the app to remove its local app data. Exported saves, device backups, and cloud backups are separate; manage those in the relevant storage service or device settings. We cannot retrieve a save stored only on your device.

The shared MinimalisticGamers policy explains GitHub Pages hosting, support retention, and policy updates. For general questions, use support without including personal information. GitHub and Apple handle requests about records they hold under their own policies.
