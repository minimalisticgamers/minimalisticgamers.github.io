The experience

A first meal. A growing colony.

Small actions build a living economy. Grow your population, put workers to work, equip a crew, and discover maps, challenges and the world beyond.

  • Gather, trap, build and hire
  • Equip a colony and explore maps
  • Preserve offline progress and backups

Includes repeated fantasy combat and randomized items exchanged for earned bones. No real-money purchases or cash-out.

Your progress

A little care for your save.

Use Settings → Save now, then Export backup to keep a JSON copy through Files. Import validates the backup and asks before replacing progress. Original save-text controls remain in the game.

Does this need an account?

No app account or developer-operated cloud-save service is enabled. Progress stays on your iPhone.

What happens while I’m away?

Original offline-progress mechanics and the AutoSave preference are preserved. Bones are earned through play; no real-money purchases, ads, app account or cloud-save service are enabled.

Is it available now?

An independent iPhone adaptation of the original Trimps 5.10.1 game. The iPhone edition is in preparation and is not available to download.
