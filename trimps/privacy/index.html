Trimps / App privacy

Your game stays yours.

Privacy policy for the independent iPhone edition.

Effective 30 October 2026. Applies to Trimps: Local Edition, iPhone adaptation version 1.0.0. This does not describe the original creator’s website or other editions.

Data not collected.

This offline iPhone edition does not collect or send your game data to the developer. It has no app account, advertising, analytics, tracking, developer-operated cloud-save service or real-money purchase integration.

Progress on your device.

The original compressed game save and an on-device native backup hold your progress and preferences. Cloud-account credentials are not included in host backups.

Removing the app can remove local progress. Export a separate backup first. Device backups and a Files destination you select may have their own storage and privacy settings.

Connections and links.

Normal play uses bundled game resources. No original online account, purchase or analytics service is active in this edition. An informational or support link opens only when you choose it; Safari and the destination website follow their own policies.

Support and this website.

If you choose to open a GitHub support issue, GitHub processes that information and the post is public. Do not post a private save or personal information. This support website uses no advertising or analytics scripts; its hosting provider may process ordinary web-request information to serve the pages.

Changes and contact.

This policy will be updated if the app’s data practices change. For privacy or support questions, open a support issue without including private information.
