Trimps / Support

A little help.

Backups, offline play and help with your iPhone game.

Player support

Tell us what happened.

Open support on GitHub

A GitHub account is required. Posts and attachments are public; leave out personal details and private save files.

Keep a separate backup.

Use Settings → Save now, then Export backup to keep a JSON copy through Files. Import validates the backup and asks before replacing progress. Original save-text controls remain in the game.

Do not remove the app until you have a separate backup. We do not have a server copy of your progress.

Playing offline.

Original offline-progress mechanics and the AutoSave preference are preserved. Bones are earned through play; no real-money purchases, ads, app account or cloud-save service are enabled.

Report a problem.

Include the game name, version/build, iPhone model, iOS version, what happened and steps to reproduce. A screenshot can help; check it for personal information before posting.

Edition and availability.

An independent iPhone adaptation of the original Trimps 5.10.1 game. The iPhone edition is in preparation and is not available to download.
