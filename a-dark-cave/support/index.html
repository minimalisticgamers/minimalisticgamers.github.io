A Dark Cave / Support

A little help.

Backups, offline play and help with your iPhone game.

Player support

Tell us what happened.

Open support on GitHub

A GitHub account is required. Posts and attachments are public; leave out personal details and private save files.

Keep a separate backup.

Open the native Settings controls to export a JSON backup through Files. Import validates a compatible backup, asks before replacement, and keeps a local safety copy when the current save can be read.

Do not remove the app until you have a separate backup. We do not have a server copy of your progress.

Playing offline.

Progress uses the original local-edition rules. Keep a separate backup before reinstalling. No cloud account, online save service, ads or real-money purchases are enabled.

Report a problem.

Include the game name, version/build, iPhone model, iOS version, what happened and steps to reproduce. A screenshot can help; check it for personal information before posting.

Edition and availability.

An independent iPhone adaptation of the original full local edition. The iPhone edition is in preparation and is not available to download.
