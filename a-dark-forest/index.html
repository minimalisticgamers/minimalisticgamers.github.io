Your progress

A little care for your save.

Play in landscape. Use Export beside the desired save in the original save selector. Copy the complete save code to a separate private location, and use Import in the selector to restore a compatible code.

Does this need an account?

No app account or developer-operated cloud-save service is enabled. Progress stays on your iPhone.

What happens while I’m away?

The original game handles its own progression and save rules. iOS can suspend an app in the background; this edition has no developer-operated server or cloud-save service.

Is it available now?

This independent iPhone project preserves the public Godot prototype labelled DEMO 1.4.6. It does not include the separate commercial Steam edition. The iPhone edition is in preparation and is not available to download.
