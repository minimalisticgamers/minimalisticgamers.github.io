Before you play.

Anxiety, panic, alcohol abuse, sexuality, strong language, and mature emotional themes. An interactive story, with no clinical or treatment claims.

The original game saves chapter checkpoints locally. Continue returns to the last saved chapter checkpoint, rather than every line of dialogue. Use the native menu to export a separate web-storage backup before removing or resetting the app.
